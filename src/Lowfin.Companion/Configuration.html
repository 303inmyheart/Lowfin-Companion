Lowfin Companion

Keys stay on this Jellyfin server. Clients choose which providers and ratings to use. Leave a field empty to disable that provider. Existing completion rules and cache data are preserved.

Cover tags · DVD / 5.1 / HDR

Optional Lowfin-style labels, drawn by Lowfin inside the visible movie and episode image frame. Original artwork stays unchanged. DVD means SD video up to 1024 × 576; 5.1 means at least one six-channel audio track. HDR indicates HDR10, HDR10+, HLG or Dolby Vision; Lowfin only displays it when HDR playback is enabled on that TV. Series and season posters show DVD when more than half their available episodes have SD/DVD resolution. Backdrops and TV logos stay untouched. 4:3 images cropped to 16:9 keep both tags fully visible.

The persistent server index updates from Jellyfin events and reconciles every 24 hours. Manual checks are available under Dashboard → Scheduled Tasks → Lowfin Companion.

Lowfin picks up changed tag settings on its next start. Only compact tag metadata is loaded on demand; cached covers and metadata are preserved.

Additional cover tags

Choose the available resolution, HDR, codec, audio and language labels. Stereo is off by default. Audio languages use compact language codes; VOST labels identify subtitle languages without a matching audio track. Lowfin shows a bounded, frame-relative layout and an overflow count when needed. HDR labels require HDR playback enabled on that TV.
